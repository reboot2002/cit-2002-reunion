Fees and how to sign up.

Registration fees

Still cheaper than the canteen bills we never cleared.

Batchmate
Rs 12,000per batchmate
Adult family / guest
Rs 3,000per additional adult
Child
Rs 1,500per child below 10 years

Example: batchmate + spouse + one child under 10 = Rs 12,000 + Rs 3,000 + Rs 1,500 = Rs 16,500

The Sunday day trip is optional and has a separate sign-up and cost.

How to register

Easier than TNEA counselling. Promise.

  1. Fill the formShare your name, email, department and whether you will attend.
  2. Hear from your coordinatorYour department coordinator will reach out through your department group.
  3. Pay your feePay the registration fee to your coordinator to complete your registration.
